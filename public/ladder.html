§1

The finding that reorganises everything else

Sorted by family, this corpus looks like broad coverage. Sorted by capital, it looks like something else entirely.

Two-thirds of the work cannot be held at the rung being climbed

681 of 1,059 specifications (64.3%) are long-short constructions — quintile spreads, decile sorts, WML, HML, pair trades. Every one requires margin and stock borrow. Neither exists in an account below roughly $10,000. That is not an emphasis problem; it is two-thirds of the research being structurally inapplicable to the account it was meant to grow.

The comfortable explanation would be that the long-only region is simply unexplored. It isn't. 267 long-only-viable specifications have been tested, and their best portfolio t is 1.58 against a deflation ceiling of 5.4555. The imbalance is a misallocation of effort, not an unexplored seam.

The structural result

Exactly one candidate has a band covering the whole ladder — and it is the benchmark every active overlay built here was measured against and lost to. Out of sample: passive SR 0.73 at 8.6%/yr; the best active variant 0.51 at 2.9%. The trend overlay is a drawdown purchase costing ~5.7pp/yr of return.

Breadth and capacity together account for 76 kills — more than cost, deflation and drift combined. This programme keeps finding effects in places too thin to hold money. That is a sharper statement than "nothing works": the effects are frequently there, and they are located where size cannot go.

§5

What would change the answer

The honest allocation of effort at L0

At the median simulated path to $1,000, $940 of the $1,000 is money contributed. A strategy twice as good acts on the remaining 6%. At the bottom of this ladder the research programme is not the binding constraint on the account — the deposit rate is.

This is uncomfortable to write inside a research programme, and it is what the simulation says.

Six forward clocks are registered, with numeric two-sided promote/kill rules written into an append-only table before their data existed. Earliest maturity is roughly twelve months. Five of the six carry a written prediction that they will fail — recorded in advance, so the calibration record survives whichever way they land.

The untested region is not a signal family. It is L3: every simulated path ran out of panel before the ~25 years that $100/mo compounding needs, so the top of the ladder is censored, not refused. Reporting that as failure would be the same error this programme has caught in itself repeatedly — and the first version of the simulator did exactly that, until censoring was separated from failure.

Figures read directly from the engine's ledger on the date stamped below. Aegis is a single-operator research programme; no signal is published, nothing is promoted to capital, and this is a synthesis of what was measured rather than advice about anyone's money.
