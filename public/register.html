§1

The position, stated first

Nothing here works yet. One thousand and twenty-one specifications have been tested across equities, futures, FX, crypto perpetuals and options. None has been promoted to real capital. Two sit at the paper rung on a forward clock and have earned nothing. The most likely terminal state of this programme is that nothing ever clears — and that is the designed outcome of a falsification engine, not its failure.

The claim this page is actually making

Not "we found an edge." The claim is narrower and checkable: every number below survived an attempt to kill it, and the ones that didn't are printed here with the same prominence as the ones that did. If you find a claim we have not already recorded against ourselves, the engine has failed at its only job.

The base rate is unforgiving — roughly 97% of retail traders lose, and under 1% beat fees over fifteen years. So the default verdict on any strategy here is REJECT, and the burden sits entirely on the strategy. The only component with near-certain positive expected value is the risk gate.

§2

What we retracted

Twenty-nine claims were pre-registered in an append-only table before their data existed. The table raises on UPDATE and DELETE, so an outcome cannot be rewritten once recorded. Seventeen of the twenty-nine were retracted. The struck line is what was claimed; the marked line is what the measurement actually said.

D-6272026-08-26

The settlement-fails signal earns −11.10%/yr at t −8.84 — the strongest result the programme has produced, after surviving ten consecutive attacks.

Relative only — the short leg rises Every bucket earns a positive return. The high-surprise bucket is +0.100% raw over 20 sessions — it goes up — and is negative in only 43 of 99 periods. True excess versus the universe is −0.218% at t −3.12, about −2.75%/yr. Roughly a quarter of the headline, reachable only by shorting the hardest-to-borrow names in the market.

D-5902026-08-26

The beta hypothesis is refuted — the short side shows a genuine timing component independent of market exposure.

False win · retracted same day Reported from a pooled number in which one stop geometry held 64.3% of all short trades. Ex-swing, the ratio is 0.07× against the pooled 1.21×. The conclusion was an artifact of aggregation. This retraction is why THE MECHANISM LAW exists.

D-6152026-08-26

Reg SHO forces close-out of persistent fails after 13 settlement days. That is compelled buying on a regulatory deadline — a mechanical, non-behavioural edge.

Sign miss — and the stated expectation was wrong I pre-registered publication timing as the likely killer. Instead the opposite happened: persistent fails do predict, but in the direction opposite to the close-out story. The mechanism was wrong in an informative way, and the pre-registration is what made that visible rather than deniable.

D-6072026-08-25

Hedging pressure predicts relative returns across futures markets, in the direction the literature documents.

The pre-registered direction loses Long-high-hedging-pressure returns −13.48%/yr, portfolio t −4.16, Sharpe −0.93 over 1,037 weeks. The mirror gives −13.47%/yr. Halves agree in both. A profitable flip exists — and is not claimable, because it was never registered.

D-6062026-08-25

Perpetuals carry the edge and spot does not — measured at SR 0.54 versus 0.11 on 31 name-matched pairs.

Reverses at honest breadth At the pre-registered ≥150-name floor (311 matched pairs) the gap inverts: perp SR 0.32 against spot SR 0.50. The original 31-pair result measured a universe, not a factor.

D-592 / D-5932026-08-26

At maker fees the sub-fee effect becomes 2.7× the round trip and clears — and the 50–121bp non-retracement return can be taken by entering at the close.

The fill is not independent of the outcome Monotone across five depths: filled 0.78–7.21bp (all t < 1.25) versus unfilled 49.67–120.84bp (t to 72.24). You are filled precisely when the move is not happening. Taking it at the close yields −9.03 to −0.99bp; t 72.24 → t −0.63.

D-6032026-08-25

A cross-section of funding rates yields 37 placeable symbols at a net 5.0–6.6%/yr carry. The snapshot passed.

Circular selection Symbols were selected on trailing-90-day funding and then their trailing-90-day funding was reported. The test measured its own selection criterion. Re-made honestly — select on the first 90 days, measure the next — it does not stand.

D-6242026-08-26

The finding is specific to settlement fails — a distinct microstructural signal about delivery failure.

Specificity retracted; general structure holds Short-volume surprise on the identical construction gives −5.14%/yr at t −6.34 across 5,814,843 events. Same sign, comparable strength. The signal was never about settlement — it is any quantity spiking against its own trailing median.

D-6282026-08-27

The deflation ceiling is 5.410 at 2,266,819 counted trials — the bar every result must clear.

A bar nothing paid for The published ceiling and the one the running agents computed were different numbers. A 734,400-trial sweep read the counter, added its spend in memory, printed an honest 5.410, and never wrote those trials down. Every later run used 5.337 — lower, and therefore more permissive. No result was falsely cleared (the highest score ever recorded is 3.73), but a control that only holds while nothing is close is not a control. Found only because this page required publishing what the research cost.

W2-noa2026-08-27

Net operating assets — one of the four factors THE COVERAGE LAW was written about — will show the documented balance-sheet-bloat premium.

A genuine null, properly powered 62 rebalances, mean breadth 576 names, above the pre-registered 500 floor. Quintiles are non-monotone. This one is evidence about the market rather than about our data — which is the distinction the whole coverage apparatus exists to preserve.

Seven claims did hold

The register is not uniformly negative, and reporting it that way would be its own distortion. Seven pre-registrations survived — among them that the fails signal is independent of short interest (correlation 0.114, opposite signs) and of trading volume (0.023), and that hedging-pressure's sign replicates out of its original asset class at t −9.26. Surviving ten attacks is what earned the fails signal an eleventh, which is the one that finally reduced it.

§3

Reproduce a claim

Three tiers, ordered by what they cost you. The first needs nothing but the repository and runs in two seconds. We state plainly where reproduction gets expensive rather than implying it is all equally easy.

Tier 1 — the honest-statistics core

No data · no network · no credentials · ~2 seconds

Deflated Sharpe, PBO, walk-forward splitting and the look-ahead contract are pure functions with no dependencies. This is the part of the engine every number downstream relies on. 

$ deno test supabase/functions/_shared/

ok | 297 passed | 0 failed (2s)

Includes the fail-closed contract test: every feature row must carry a parseable effective_date, so a backtest cannot read a value before it was legally knowable.

Tier 2 — break a guard on purpose

Local Postgres + PostgREST · minutes

Eighteen guards enforce the laws in §4. Every one was verified to go RED before its green was trusted — a guard that has only ever passed is an untested guard. Each ships a self-test that must fail on a synthetic violation and pass on honest input: 

$ SELFTEST=1 deno run --allow-net --allow-env --allow-read \
    scripts/trial-ledger-guard.ts

  live counter: N = 2,265,801  ->  ceiling sqrt(2 ln N) = 5.4099
  static scan flags an unpaid SPENDER: YES
  static scan SPARES a read-only reporter: YES
  SELFTEST PASSED — fails on an unpaid ceiling, passes on a paid one.

The second assertion exists because this guard's first version failed it — it flagged correct read-only code. A guard that REDs on working code gets switched off, so both directions are tested permanently.

Tier 3 — reproduce a measured result end to end

Full ingest from public sources · hours to days

Every dataset in §1 has an ingest script in scripts/ and every source is free. Reproduction is genuinely available, and it is genuinely slow — the FTD table alone is 10.8M rows across 39,316 symbols. A worked example, scored on a window that was already measured independently: 

$ bash scripts/ingest-ftd.sh          # SEC fails-to-deliver, free
$ FROM_D=2024-01-01 LIQUID_ONLY=1 \
    deno run --allow-net --allow-env --allow-write \
    scripts/ftd-persistence.ts

  376,597 events, 30 monthly periods, mean breadth 1784, LIQUID HALF
  BOOK long-most-persistent: -22.05%/yr   PORTFOLIO t -4.88

That figure matches an earlier independent measurement to two decimals. It is also the number D-627 above reduces by roughly 75% once measured against the universe instead of in isolation — both are in the register, and the reduction is the one that counts.

What you cannot reproduce, stated plainly

Results depending on paid data are not reproducible here, because we have not bought any — options surface history, intraday equities and borrow rates remain unpurchased. Eight coverage gaps are open and listed in GAP_REGISTER.md with the specific verdict each one blocks. Where an input was never fetched, the verdict is recorded as UNTESTED, never as a null result about the market.

§4

Sixteen laws, each bought with an error

None of these were imported from a textbook. Every one was written the day a specific measured claim turned out to be wrong, and every one is enforced by a machine guard rather than by intention.

Coverage

A null is evidence about the market only if the data could have detected the effect. Otherwise it is evidence about our data. Bought when program-level "no edge" conclusions were drawn while holding 5 of hundreds of available EDGAR concepts.

Liquidity

The promotable number is the liquid tercile's, never the pooled one. Bought when 11–19%/yr headlines decomposed into 20–40%/yr illiquid and 0.9–5.7%/yr liquid.

Effect size

Significance says "is it there". Only multiples of the round-trip cost say "is it worth acting on". Bought on a signal with negative rank IC on 20 of 20 instruments — at 0.02–0.14× the fee.

Breadth

A cross-section under ~50 names is UNTESTED, whatever its t-stat. Bought on 94.2%/yr at SR 1.13 computed on fourteen instruments; at proper breadth, SR 0.34.

Execution

A maker fee is a hypothesis about fills, not a cost model. Measure the return conditional on filling. Bought when the entire positive return lived in the days the order never filled.

Same-bar corollary

A close-derived signal may not act at that close. Bought when the first four specs ever to clear all six gates sign-flipped from +19%/yr to −11%/yr under one day of lag.

Selection

Choosing components on the full sample makes the result in-sample, whatever the split says. Bought on a "selective overlay worth 0.37 Sharpe" that, re-chosen on train only, qualified in zero classes.

Universe

A Sharpe that doubles across defensible universe definitions is NOT IDENTIFIED. Bought on one strategy printing SR 0.61 to 1.30 across five equally defensible definitions of itself.

Sign

A direction asserted is not a direction checked, and a post-hoc flip is never claimable. Bought when the best result in the programme turned out to be the registered direction run backwards.

Instrument

A premium must be measured in the instrument that would hold it. Bought four separate times, each by a different mechanism, each discovered after the claim was made.

Holdability

Depth is not the risk; duration is. State the longest time underwater. Bought on a candidate that survived ten statistical attacks and was killed by 1,363 days underwater.

Mechanism

A causal story requires pre-registration or the label DESCRIPTIVE ONLY. Bought on D-590 above — seven mechanism stories proposed in one day, seven dead.

Pre-commitment

A forward test without a written promote/kill rule is an option to rationalise later. Bought when four forward clocks were found running with no stated decision rule.

Precondition

Every run prints its effective configuration and refuses to start on a near-miss variable name. Bought on nine self-inflicted defects, including a self-test that silently did nothing for days.

Continuity

A matured forward clock without a verdict turns the board red. Bought because a clock nobody scores is indistinguishable from no clock.

Guards inspect running code

A guard on the record does not constrain the agents. Bought with seven green guards and three live production defects, one surfacing a false clear for nine consecutive cycles.
