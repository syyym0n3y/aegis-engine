What we found

  • No chart/timing signal survives honest testing — ~1.01M conditional strategies across the tradeable universe, deflated by trial count. Zero cleared.
  • Risk management is the durable edge — sane sizing keeps an account alive even trading a losing strategy. That's the near-certain positive-expectancy component.
  • Compounding comes from the global factor book (Sharpe ~1), not setups.
  • Macro measures fragility, not direction — the overlay only ever shrinks size when the system is primed to break.
  • The one cyclic regularity is the BTC halving (halving→top ≈530d, n=3) — pre-registered as a forward prediction, not backfit.
  • Rauf is right about one thing: the edge fires in specific conditions, not every day. Overtrading is how you give it back. Aegis quantifies exactly which conditions — and proves most "conditions" are noise.
