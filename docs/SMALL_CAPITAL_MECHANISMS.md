# THE SMALL-CAPITAL MECHANISMS — what this record measured as REAL, assembled (D-834, 2026-09-08)

> Every mechanism below was measured on this stack and found **real**, then dismissed individually as too small.
> Nobody had ever put them in one place. For an account starting near zero that is backwards, because several of them
> are **capacity-inverted**: they exist precisely because institutions cannot participate at their size, so they are
> not competed away — and "negligible" was a judgement made against a large account, not against this one.
>
> **This is a synthesis of existing measurements, not a new claim.** No number here is new; each cites the entry that
> produced it. Nothing here is promoted, and the UK-accessibility column is the part that decides whether any of it is
> real *for this operator* — and most of it is UNVERIFIED, which is stated rather than assumed (THE INSTRUMENT LAW,
> whose four consecutive failures were all exactly this gap).

## 4. What this assembly does and does not say

**It does not say these add up to a living.** Section 1's two viable mechanisms, at the capital they need, produce
something in the low thousands per year at best, and both hinge on a broker question nobody has answered.

**What it does say** is sharper than that. Every mechanism in section 1 pays for a *structural* reason — priority
granted by rule, capacity below an institutional minimum — not for being right about direction. That is the only
category on this entire record that has survived measurement, and it is the category the programme spent 2.9M trials
NOT looking at. The engine hunted forecasting edges and found none; the things it found real, it found by accident
while looking elsewhere, and then dismissed for being small.

**The one action this justifies:** answer the broker-access question for odd-lot tenders and pre-deal SPAC commons
before spending another session on forecasting. It is a question about a UK brokerage account, not about markets, and
it decides whether section 1 is worth anything at all. Until it is answered, section 1 is a research-space finding
exactly like every other one on this record — and THE INSTRUMENT LAW's four failures say that assuming the conversion
is how this programme has been wrong every previous time.
