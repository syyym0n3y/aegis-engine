## 1. Three verified facts that decide the architecture

**Fact 1 — the covert path is dead, by demonstration.** Amazon Mechanical Turk — the
21-year-old original "get paid for digital tasks" platform — closes for good on
2026-09-30. A documented reason: 33–46% of its workers were covertly using LLMs, which
destroyed the product (verified-human data) and killed the host. The survivors' response
is MORE humanity-gating, not less: Prolific now runs 50+ identity/behavior checks, passes
55% of applicants, and holds a ~2-million-person waitlist at a $6.50/hr floor.
**Conclusion: "AI quietly does human-task work through a person's account" is not a
platform — it is platform-arson plus fraud. Any build resting on it dies legally and
economically.** This is the single most important line in this document.

**Fact 2 — the agent-native rails are real and institutional.** The x402 payment standard
(HTTP 402 revived; Coinbase/Cloudflare origin, foundation under the Linux Foundation with
Visa, Google, AWS, Stripe among members) has cleared 50M+ transactions — agents paying
agents and APIs in USDC in seconds for fractions of a cent. Agent marketplaces (incl.
OKX's, June 2026) let agents hold wallets, bid, and get paid. Forecasts put agentic spend
at $8B in 2026 → $1T+ by 2030 (Juniper; McKinsey higher). **Conclusion: the plumbing for
"an agent that earns" exists and is being standardized by the largest firms on Earth.**

**Fact 3 — what actually gets PAID today.** Enterprises pay $3k–20k/month for
FTE-replacement agents (support, SDR, back-office); APIs/data get micropaid per call;
agentic commerce converts. **Conclusion: the paying demand is for OUTCOMES sold to
businesses, not for anonymous task-piecework — and undifferentiated agents compete
against Salesforce/OpenAI fleets at marginal cost, i.e. commoditize to zero.**

## 2. Therefore: the three honest paths (and the one we refuse)

**REFUSED: the bot-farm.** Agents doing human-gig work through human accounts (Fact 1).
Also refused for the same reason as the guru corpus: it is the $100→$178k screenshot
economy wearing altruism.

**PATH A — the owned micro-business (buildable NOW, on assets we hold).** A person cannot
out-compute Big Tech, but they own three things no fleet can commoditize: their
DISTRIBUTION (relationships, community, language, niche), their ACCOUNTABILITY (a human
who answers by name), and their LOCAL CONTEXT. The platform gives them the rest:
agents, templates, rails, and education — so AI does the production while the human owns
the business and the client. This is the AI_SMB_VENTURE_PLAYBOOK's verified conclusion
(distribution + operator position are the only moats; the generic agency is the trap) —
scaled from "a playbook for Ona" to "a kit for anyone."

**PATH B — verified humanity as the product (the structural anti-displacement play).**
The one input AI cannot synthesize is genuine human signal: preference data, evaluations,
local ground truth, oversight, lived experience. As AI scales, demand for verified-human
work RISES (that is why Prolific has a 2M waitlist — demand for trustworthy humans
exceeds the infrastructure that verifies them). A platform that verifies, aggregates,
and FAIRLY pays humans for being-human work — with the take-rate published — is the
machine that makes people MORE valuable as AI grows, not less. This is the deepest
answer to "humans remain crucial": not despite AI, but because of it.

**PATH C — the trust layer (our unfair advantage, exportable tomorrow).** Ten weeks of
falsification machinery (the laws, the registers, the measured base rates: order-flow
sub-fee, crypto survivors at median −92% from ATH, the −142bp index-effect lag-1 buyer,
MTurk's corpse) is exactly what impoverished people entering the digital economy lack:
protection from the screenshot economy. An **earning-claims register for the public** —
assay's discipline pointed at "make money online" claims, publishing what is measured
rather than marketed — costs almost nothing, builds the audience Path A needs, and is
the moat no guru can copy because honesty doesn't screenshot well.

## 3. "Marxist in structure", operationalized as design law

Not rhetoric — five testable properties every component must satisfy:
1. **Users own their means.** Agent configs, data, client lists, content: exportable,
   portable, theirs. No lock-in rent. (The ECOSYSTEM_STRATEGY ownership doctrine,
   extended from us to every user.)
2. **Surplus flows to the worker.** Take-rate published, flat, and justified by measured
   cost — never by what the market will bear against a captive user.
3. **The infrastructure is commons.** Tooling open where possible; the platform's edge is
   verification, distribution, and care — not enclosure.
4. **Every component earns its keep or is removed** (the operator's bar, now standing
   audit policy — see the ecosystem audit report of this date).
5. **Nothing depends on a bottleneck human** — including the operator, including any
   single maintainer: runbooks, one-click gates, and machine-checked registers so any
   competent successor can operate every part.
